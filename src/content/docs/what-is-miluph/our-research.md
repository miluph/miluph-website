title: Numerical Particle Methods Group
---

The Numerical Particle Methods Group is part of the Computational Physics group, formerly led by the late Prof. Dr. Wilhelm Kley, at the University of Tübingen, now managed by Prof. Dr. Chritoph Schäfer. 

Our research focuses on meshfree particle methods—such as Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH), the Material Point Method (MPM), Molecular Dynamics (MD), and hierarchical Tree algorithms—and their application to complex astrophysical processes.

### Current Research Topics

- Computational physics and high-performance computing (HPC)
- Meshfree particle methods (SPH and MPM)
- Planetesimal growth and protoplanetary processes
- Hypervelocity impacts and collisions
- Formation and evolution of binary asteroids
- ESA's **Hera** space mission
