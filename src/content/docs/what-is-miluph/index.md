---
title: One Framework for Multiple Codes
---

The **miluph** framework integrates multiple numerical particle methods to simulate solids and fluids. It currently encompasses two Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH) implementations: **miluphCUDA** for single NVIDIA GPU executionn, and **miluphHPC** for distributed multi-GPU simulations across multiple compute nodes.

In addition to SPH, the framework includes a Material Point Method (MPM) solver **SmashMPM** as an alternative continuum-mechanics approach. 

Future releases will further expand the framework with additional numerical methods.
